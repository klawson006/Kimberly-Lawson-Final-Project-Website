"Color Me" wesbite!

Weirdly enough, this was the site I had the most fun making even though it is extremely simple and took the least amount of time compared to all my other sites. I suppose it was the customization aspects that made it feel unique and a nice change of pace, letting the users decorate the site to their own liking instead of making it specifically for myself. Using the reccomended AI tools to debug the code was really useful too, because it is very easy to make errors, as I had to watch tutorials for color fade transitions. In general, a fun and simple assignment.
