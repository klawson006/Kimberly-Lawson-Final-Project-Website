Personal Portfolio website

This is a informal personal portfolio site I made for my midterm, and feels the most like my personality. Compared to all my other projects, this one feels the most like myself (because its supposed to be), and though painstaking to figure out Dreamweaver, it turned out nicely. It is honestly one of the best websites I ever made, and I may possibly update this in my free time with new things as I continue to learn more about web production. For the site you're on right now, I really did want to just make the same version but for my class projects, including reusing all of my hand-drawn gifs, but I decided not to in order to show my commitment to making something different and still valuable. In a way, my class website portfolio is like the opposite twin to my personal one. While my personal site is heavily decorated and has a more relaxed, chill vibe, I went with making my class site a lot more simplified and formal, so that my more decorated sites stand out, establishing them as the focus of what this place is about.
