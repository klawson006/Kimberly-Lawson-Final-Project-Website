Unique Flora

Around the time that I created this website, spring time had newly arrived. It may have played a factor in the flora theme of this project, aside from learning about unique flowers whilst scrolling Pinterest one day. I decided to combine early 2000s quirky webpages with a modern and sleek design to make something that had the best of both worlds. Admitedly, sometimes I'm lazy and only want to make pretty visuals instead of coding, so I enjoyed the fact that Readymag does most of the heavy lifting of code while a user can just decorate to their heart's content. The world has not been the greatest in this day and age, so I thought that making a nice website that shows pictures and facts about beautiful plants and trees on our Earth would be a great mood booster. I enjoyed researching what I was interested in and then showing it to the world on this unique site (pun intended).
