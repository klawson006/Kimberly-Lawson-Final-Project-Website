Final Thoughts

I was never a coding genius in the past, and my first year at Hunter was less than great trying to be a computer science major (I've long sinced switched to emerging media, thank god). I was an artist by heart growing up, so knowing what code goes where has never came easy to me. However, it just took simply joining this course and a visual communication course in the past to figure out that I could both code better and make it look pretty at the same time. To be honest, I was not a fan of how we jumped from one medium of web production to another in what felt like a short amount of time. But it did help expose me to sites and programs I would probably not have heard about in the past, and I am grateful for that. In fact, it has motivated me to start coding more in my free time and seeing the many possibilities at my fingertips with html, java, and css. Improvement takes time, and thanks to the material we've covered throughout this semster, I've got a good sense of website production and the materials available at the click of a button. This site is a visual log of what I've learned, what I could build upon, and the potential to apply it to my future.
