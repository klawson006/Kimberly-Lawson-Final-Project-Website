"it was so easy once.."

I remember when I could barely spell the word "determine" in elementary, and now I'm a college student still trying to navigate life and figure out what works best for me. This VR experience is a loop of fragments of floating shapes and glitching text, made to represent the fragments of childhood memories that one still remembers years later. Some might take it as sad, creepy, or even nothing at all, because this vr experience leaves the user up to their own interpretation. I liked working with VR the least out of all the projects in this semester because I encountered a lot of issues with things not working. I searched why the background did not appear, and apparently it had something to do with my browser, though I have no clue how true that is. Regardless, I wanted to show that I made a big effort and I worked with what I had. And after coming up with an interesting concept, I think this project has started to grow on me.
